Anushka Parikh is a graphic designer, researcher, and artist. She is currently pursuing a BFA in Graphic Design and a Concentration in Computation at the Rhode Island School of Design. Her work largely revolves around interaction, accessible design, research, & brand identities. She is now designing at Design Guild @ RISD.


She is constantly fascinated by digital cultures, manifestos, accessible design, cybernetics, glyphs, alt text, physical computation, textiles, rocks, web poetry, em dashes, and piracy.
