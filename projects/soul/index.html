BETTER WORLD X DESIGN SOUL CONFERENCE
(personal project) website design + 3D modeling + package design
In → Fall 2023

Soul is a conference hosted by Better World annually with a different theme each year. The 2025 conference gathered speakers to discuss interdisciplinary practices in design and their relation to the soul.

As part of the graphics and web team, I was tasked with the designing the conference website, merchandise, and marketing materials that demonstrate the soul.
