COCREATE BRANDING BOOK
(internship) brand identity
In → Spring 2025

At Cocreate, I designed for the brand identity with the intent of reimagining a parent brand for Design Buddies and UXGO that provides collaboration and advising for clients in the design industry. This meant that the brand had to compliment both established brands while still maintaining a independent identity. Through this brand design, I decided to experiment with how the letters "co" can be designed to function as a visual and written logo.
